# Skiff Slant — design notes

## Intent

Slanted monoline sans for UI labels and short prose.

## Construction

- Lineage: `ROUND`, a skeleton alphabet drawn for the Sixth Cohort and shared by several
  families; each family is a different construction treatment of it.
- ROUND stroked at 66 units, round caps/dots, sheared +0.2 (about 11 degrees). The font is a slanted design in a Regular style: no italic flags or italic angle are set.

## Practical intent (not measured)

- Labels and status text, 14-24 px equivalent.

## Limits

- Not flagged as italic, so applications will not offer it as the italic of another family. Slanted forms are simple shears, not redrawn italics.
- Sibling families on the same skeleton share letter structure; they are treatments, not
  independent alphabets.
- Outline proofs only: no hinting, Windows acceptance, legibility or accessibility study.
- Authored without reference to any existing typeface's outlines.
