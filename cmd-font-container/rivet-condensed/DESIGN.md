# Rivet Condensed — design notes

## Intent

Bold, flat-ended condensed sans for narrow columns and tables.

## Construction

- Lineage: `CONDENSED`, a skeleton alphabet drawn for the Sixth Cohort and shared by three
  families; each family is a different construction treatment of it, as in the Third Cohort.
- Condensed chamfered skeleton stroked at 100 units, flat caps, bevelled joins, square dots; 44-unit bearings, tabular figures in a 400 cell.

## Practical intent (not measured)

- Narrow columns, tags and dense labels at 16-32 px equivalent.

## Limits

- Heavy strokes close small counters (`8`, `B`, `e`, `a`) at small sizes; no lighter weight exists.
- Sibling families on the same skeleton share letter structure; they are treatments, not
  independent alphabets.
- Outline proofs only: no hinting, Windows acceptance, legibility or accessibility study.
- Authored without reference to any existing typeface's outlines.
