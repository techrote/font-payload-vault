# Agent instructions

Read [README.md](README.md) in full before making, changing or naming any font. It is the brief, house style and definition of done.
It links to the two required companions: [docs/rating-system.md](docs/rating-system.md) and [docs/naming-guide.md](docs/naming-guide.md).

Non-negotiables: textural/onomatopoeic names with no colour names; Windows-installable output; one-command reproducible builds;
automated verification; honest reporting of what wasn't tested; work on a branch and hand over a PR link.
